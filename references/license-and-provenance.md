# Source and output provenance

## Answer commercial-use questions directly

Check the current official license or provider terms for the actual asset and its intended use. Give the conclusion first and link to the evidence. “Subject to service terms” on its own leaves the user's question unanswered.

| Status | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| 可商用 / Allowed | The applicable terms permit the stated use and there is no identified source permission or other condition left to resolve. |
| 有条件可商用 / Allowed with conditions | The stated use is permitted if the user meets a specific condition. Say exactly what to do, such as retaining a notice, obtaining permission, or buying the appropriate license before use. |
| 不可商用 / Prohibited for this use | The applicable terms expressly exclude the requested use. Identify the restriction. |
| 暂无法确认 / Unverified | Evidence is missing or conflicting. Identify which source, permission, or term needs verification. |

For an AI-generated PNG of a generic subject made from text alone, with no identified third-party material or mark, the provider's confirmed output terms can support a direct answer for ordinary commercial UI use. For built-in OpenAI image generation, verify the [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/#content) or the applicable [business agreement](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/). Example: **可商用（付费 App 界面图标）**。纯文字生成，未使用第三方素材，无需另购素材授权。 When the requested use needs exclusivity, trademark registration, or specific third-party rights, assess that use separately. Ordinary commercial use and exclusive rights have different requirements.

For mixed deliveries, give each asset or group with identical terms its own status. List the assets available for the stated commercial use first, then name any excluded assets and their reasons. Keep a user's input photo and independently generated output distinct. If the rights to distribute the photo are unverified, mark the photo accordingly even if the generated icon is usable under its own terms.

## Record the evidence

Record source rights separately from generation output terms. A source may be verified commercially usable with conditions, paid/permission required, explicitly noncommercial, or unconfirmed. AI-generated output is a different property; it is not an open-source license or a promise of exclusivity.

For uploaded images, record user ownership/permission statements and any verifiable license evidence. No evidence means unconfirmed, not noncommercial. A known icon match does not establish rights for an entire screenshot. For commercial-ready packages, exclude unresolved and noncommercial source chains; a warning does not convert them to cleared assets.

Distinguish: (1) photo viewed to identify a generic object, (2) image supplied for shape/editing, (3) image supplied for material. Restrictions/uncertainty must be evaluated for the actual use; do not automatically label all outputs noncommercial because an identification-only photo has unknown rights. Do not claim omitting the photo from generation guarantees zero risk.

For each output, record filename/dimensions, subject, channels actually searched or none, source URL/license/creator or no sourced asset, every image input and role, calibration image used or prompt-only, source-rights evidence/conditions separately from output-service terms, and actual quality checks. Use compact human-readable records; no requirement to show a large table to the user.

Calibration input provenance: [resources/PROVENANCE.md](../resources/PROVENANCE.md). If it was used, say “文字描述＋内置 AI 材质参考图”; if no image input was used, say “文字生成，无图片输入”. Preserve identification-only photo records separately.
